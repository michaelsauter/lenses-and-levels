Welcome to The Lenses & Levels Architecture Documentation Model

This documentation framework proposes a novel approach to software architecture documentation, combining different "lenses" of architectural concerns with consistent "levels" of detail for each lens. This two-dimensional structure aims to provide clarity and relevance to various audiences, from newcomers to deep implementers.

The Core Idea

Traditional architecture documentation often struggles with overwhelming detail or lack of specific focus. The "Lenses & Levels" framework addresses this by organizing documentation around two key axes:

  • Lenses: Each lens represents a distinct architectural concern, such as System, Capability, Data, Security, etc. This allows stakeholders to focus on the aspects most relevant to their interests without distraction.
  • Levels: For each lens, information is structured across three consistent levels of detail: Introduction, Orientation, and Elaboration. This ensures that the documentation scales with the reader's needs, starting with simple mental models and ending with exhaustive specifications.

Benefits of the Model

Targeted Information

Readers quickly find information relevant to their role and current context, without being bogged down by irrelevant detail.

Consistent Structure

A predictable structure across all lenses makes the entire directory incredibly easy to navigate, master, and audit.

Frictionless Scalability

Accommodates various levels of detail and links directly to living artifacts (APIs, Code, Terraform) instead of duplication.

Complete Coverage

Dedicated lenses ensure that critical architectural concerns (like cost, compliance, team topology) are never overlooked.
